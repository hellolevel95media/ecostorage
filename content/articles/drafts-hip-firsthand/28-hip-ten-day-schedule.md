Every HIP unit receives a printed schedule. This article reads one, from an HDB flat that went through HIP, and explains what each day involves in practice. Contact details and signatures on the posted copy have been blurred.

Your own schedule may differ, because internal work items can change within the ten days. The sequence is still a useful picture of what to expect, especially with our notes on which days were the worst.

[[photo: misc/checklist-clean.jpg | The posted 10-day unit schedule. Our flat during HIP.]]

## The ten days

The schedule runs on working days and skips Sundays.

**Day 1.** Protection of furniture and floor, with a curtain door at the kitchen toilet and master bedroom walkway. Fixtures and fittings are dismantled, window protection goes up, and the existing floor and wall tiles are hacked. A temporary toilet is installed in the unit. An owner or an adult must be present.

**Day 2.** Floor traps and fittings are replaced, grout is filled in at the toilet and kitchen stack pipe area, and the ceiling is skimmed.

**Day 3.** Corners are shaped, non-shrink grout goes onto the floor and upturn, and the aluminium vent is replaced.

**Day 4.** Two coats of waterproofing membrane go onto the floor, one in the morning and one in the afternoon, followed by protective grout.

**Day 5.** The first water test is conducted, and a waterproofing screed is laid.

**Day 6.** Plastering of the toilet wall.

**Day 7.** Wall tiling begins, and the wrought-iron gate is replaced where applicable.

**Day 8.** Floor tiling, a ramp measurement if applicable, and painting of repaired concrete areas. The aluminium bi-fold door is measured.

**Day 9.** The bi-fold door, pedestal pan, basin and grab bar go in, and electrical works are reinstated. Ceilings and pipes are painted, the temporary toilet is removed, and the main timber door is replaced. Defects are rectified and cleaned up, followed by the first inspection by the consultant's representative.

**Day 10.** The second water test, final paint touch-up and cleaning. Any EASE items such as ramps or slip-resistance treatment are installed, followed by the final inspection and handover.

## Which days were hardest

For us, the first week was the worst, and the first one to two weeks of works in their unit were the toughest overall. Hacking happens early, and because contractors do one toilet at a time, the dust from the first toilet is still in the air when the second begins. The living room filled with a fine, misty dust that was obvious when the ceiling lights went on, and it stung the eyes, nose and throat.

A schedule line that says "hacking" does not describe that. Our response was to leave. Family members stayed with in-laws, partners and grandparents, and those who stayed to supervise rotated daytime shifts.

## Why the noise lasts longer than ten days

The ten-day schedule covers your unit. The block takes much longer. HIP proceeds in a rolling pattern: when your unit is done and waiting for inspection, the crew moves to another unit, and the drilling continues until every unit in the block is finished. In our block that took months. The first two weeks of your own works are the hardest, but sound and vibration from neighbouring units carry on well after that.

## What else the schedule shows

- **The water test happens twice.** The first comes after waterproofing and the second just before handover, giving two chances to find a leak. See [HIP Toilet Water Test](/resources/hip-toilet-water-test-opt-out).
- **Doors and gates change late.** The gate is replaced on Day 7 and the main door on Day 9, so for part of the works the entrance has less protection than usual. See [Strangers Walking Into Your Flat During HIP](/resources/strangers-walking-into-flat-during-hip).
- **An adult must be present on certain days.** The Remarks column marks them.
- **The temporary toilet arrives on Day 1 and leaves on Day 9.**

## Frequently asked questions

### How many working days does HIP take inside a unit?
The schedule in our flat covered ten working days.

### Which days of HIP are the hardest?
For us, the first one to two weeks, when the hacking and drilling produce the most dust and noise.

### Why does the drilling go on after my unit is finished?
HIP works proceed in a rolling pattern, and the crew moves to the next unit, so drilling continues until the whole block is done. In our block that took months.

### When is the water test?
This schedule included a first water test on Day 5 and a second on Day 10.

## Summary

- A HIP unit schedule lists the work for each of ten working days.
- Hacking and dust peak in the first week or two, and inspections come at the end.
- Drilling from neighbouring units continues for months while the crew works through the block.
- The water test appears twice, on Day 5 and Day 10 in this schedule.
