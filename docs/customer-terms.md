# Customer terms: owner-confirmed facts for articles and FAQs

Source: Jonny, 25 Nov 2026 (answers to 14 likely customer questions). Use this as the fact source when writing articles, FAQ blocks and service-page copy, so the owner is not asked again. Update it when a term changes. Items under INTERNAL never go in public copy.

## Space and price
- 1 pallet = 1 module = 20 sqft = flat S$60 per month, whatever the order size (no volume tiers).
- A 3-room flat's worth of belongings is about 8 to 10 pallets, so about S$480 to S$600 a month.
- Exact count: a no-obligation site survey (contact us).
- `calcspec.md` deliberately keeps lower truckload pallet figures (4 to 6). Do not change it, and do not repeat that figure in articles.

## Period, extension, ending
- Minimum rental is 1 month. After that the plan continues and is billed automatically, so a delayed renovation needs no action.
- Ending early incurs no additional fee.

## Taking items out, notice, visits
- Partial extraction is allowed at any point in the storage period. Depending on the plan it may be included.
- The customer gives up to 72 hours' notice before a pickup or delivery. This is a notice requirement, never a delivery-time guarantee.
- Site visits: contact us to request site visit clearance. No walk-ins and no street address in marketing.

## Collection and handling
- Collection from condos (including lift booking) and landed homes: yes.
- Each customer's items go on their own recycled wooden pallet and are shrink-wrapped. This prevents cross-contamination with other customers' items and keeps everything from falling off in transit.
- Packing: customer packs, or we provide it as an add-on service on request.

## Cannot be stored
Food, flammables, chemicals, living things (plants and pets included) and all contraband under Singapore law.

## Payment
- PayNow and credit card.
- A deposit is required to confirm a job order (amount not recorded here).
- The first payment is due before items are picked up.
- Storage fees run from the day items are inbounded to a storage facility, not from booking.

HIP10, RENO10, MOVE10, BTO10, STUDENT10, BIZ10: 10% off the first month's storage, max 100 claims each. HIP10 and RENO10 confirmed live by Jonny on 25 Nov; all six validated live on 17 Nov (see `HANDOFF.md` section 0).
